### 🧬 MyoGenomics — Computational Genomics & Proteomics Platform

A computational bioinformatics application focused on genomic and proteomic data analysis, with particular emphasis on muscle development.

The project aims to integrate genomic sequence processing, protein translation, biochemical analysis, and molecular structure visualization into an interactive platform.

**Current Development Focus**
- Retrieval of genomic information from external biological databases
- DNA sequence processing and normalization
- Extraction and analysis of specific DNA sequences
- Translation of nucleotide sequences into amino acid sequences
- Interactive visualization of DNA and protein structures

**Planned Research & Development**
- Amino acid composition and biochemical property analysis
- Protein molecular weight and isoelectric point calculations
- Classification of amino acids based on their chemical properties
- Analysis of peptide bonds and protein organization
- Exploration of primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary protein structures
- Interactive 3D protein visualization
- Investigation of proteins associated with skeletal muscle development

**External Biological Resources:** Ensembl · UniProt · AlphaFold Protein Structure Database

**Tech Stack:** Python · Streamlit · Biopython · Pandas · NumPy · py3Dmol · Plotly

**Project Status:** In Development
